Results data supplied and used to generate the World Para Swimming Ranking Lists are subject to verification with the World Para Swimming Classification Master List.
Some errors may have occurred during the transfer or reformatting of results data. Therefore, if you encounter incorrect or missing information please contact World Para Swimming at info@WorldParaSwimming.org. To make it easier to follow your request, please provide first name, last name, country and clearly identify the competition it relates to. Thank you.

Rankings or qualification lists with a user defined period are always unofficial!
